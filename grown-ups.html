For grown-ups

Made for children 3 and up, best enjoyed together.

How it works

  • Each episode is one everyday fix, about a minute long.
  • Watch it as a video, or play it: your child taps to help Evan scan, choose the right tool and finish the job.
  • There is no way to lose. A wrong choice simply gets a gentle "try again".
  • Every episode ends with a one-line fixer tip you can repeat at home.

Safety first

  • Episodes show children what they can safely help with, and when to call a grown-up.
  • Light bulbs, sockets and tools always involve a grown-up in the story.
  • Please supervise any real fix at home.

Privacy

  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies for analytics.
  • No accounts and no sign-up forms. Nothing your child does here is collected.
  • Progress (which episodes are finished) is saved only on your own device.
